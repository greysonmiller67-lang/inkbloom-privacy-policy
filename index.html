Privacy Policy for Inkbloom

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Inkbloom ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using Inkbloom, you agree to this policy.

Inkbloom is a calm, offline logic puzzle game. The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and makes no network requests of its own. The only third-party service that processes data is the advertising provider described below.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress and settings — solved levels, stars, unlocked packs, and preferences (sound, music, vibration, color assist, reduce motion) — are saved only on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us, and we cannot access it. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Reset all progress, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Sharing your artwork

If you tap Share on a finished mandala, Inkbloom saves a picture of it into the app's own temporary folder and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice — Inkbloom does not upload it anywhere and does not keep a copy once you leave the screen. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Inkbloom is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To provide and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain information, which may include:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as your Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device information (device model, operating system, language, and similar technical data)
  • App interaction data used to serve, limit, and measure ads

This information is collected and used by Google to deliver and personalize advertising, limit the number of times you see an ad, and measure ad performance. We do not receive this personal data ourselves.

For details on how Google uses this data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your ad choices

You can control ad personalization and reset your Advertising ID in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising

Opting out of personalized ads does not remove ads; it means the ads you see may be less relevant.

Children

Inkbloom is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ads service, please contact us and we will work with the provider to address it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The app itself shares no data. The advertising data described above is collected and processed by Google as an independent service provider in accordance with its own privacy policy.

Data retention and security

Game data is stored locally on your device for as long as the app is installed and is removed when you reset progress or uninstall the app. Because the app holds no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
