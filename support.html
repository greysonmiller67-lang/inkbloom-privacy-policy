Inkbloom Support

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Inkbloom is a calm mandala puzzle. You are given a fixed set of ink stamps and you fill the artwork with them — but ink bleeds through damp paper and stops at ink that has already dried, so the order you print in decides whether you have enough.

Contact us

Questions, bug reports, or feedback are all welcome, and a real person reads every message. Email greysonmiller67@gmail.com and we normally reply within a few days.

It helps a great deal if you can include your device model, whether you are on iOS or Android, and the pack and level number if the problem happens on a particular puzzle.

Common questions

I ran out of stamps and cannot finish the level.
That is part of the puzzle rather than a bug. Each level gives a fixed budget, and printing in the wrong order can strand you — filling the middle of a damp run cuts it in two, and the rest then costs more. Undo is free and unlimited, so step back and try a different order, or use the hint button for a suggested move.

Which shapes are damp?
Damp regions are drawn with a dashed outline; dry ones are solid. Ink spreads through damp paper and stops at dry ink.

Pieces that touch fill together.
Shapes that touch each other form one region and take a single print. Shapes that stand apart each need their own. The outline shows you which is which.

My progress was reset after an update.
If you had the older grid version of Inkbloom, the update replaced every puzzle in the game, so previous solves could not carry over — the level numbers now mean completely different puzzles. The packs you had unlocked stay unlocked. This is a one-time change and does not happen on later updates.

I cannot hear the music.
Check Settings → Music, then your device's volume and silent switch. The ambient track is deliberately quiet.

Colour assist does not do anything.
It only applies to the colour packs (Stained Glass, Aurora, Cosmos), which use palettes with similar hues. Lotus and Tide paint a single gradient, so there is nothing to tell apart.

How do I erase my data?
Settings → Reset all progress clears everything on the device. Progress is stored only on your device and never leaves it — see the Privacy Policy.

Accessibility.
Settings offers Reduce motion (simplifies the bloom animation, and also follows your system setting) and Colour assist (marks each region with dots showing which ink it takes, so the puzzle never depends on colour vision).
